# NEW468: hand cranked cream separator

Three LODs, each in BLEND, FBX and GLB (nine model files). Any accompanying PNG files are original required texture support. Keep PNGs beside the models. These are original visual props, not certified functional products.

## Limitations
Cream separator based on same-product references nominal bowl diameter365mm height535mm; width, hidden backside, bottom and gears inferred. Only 0–35° crank animation tested in 49 finite samples; 360° fails: 115mm ground penetration and spout collision at 17 sampled angles. Not a full rotation or real mechanical safety certification. Hollow sleeve rear is obstructed by body 3.6–3.8mm, not through-bore of complete assembly; grip separate independent rotation not certified. Geometry normal/UV/PBR and moving/fixed surface checks independently verified with tolerance, exclusions. LOD2 rebuilt symmetric mesh removes shading banding; FBX workbench metallic/roughness differs although Cycles/Eevee original shader visually similar, exact pixel identity not asserted.
