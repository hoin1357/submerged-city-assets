# NEW471: pedestal 16mm silent film projector

Three LODs, each in BLEND, FBX and GLB (nine model files). Any accompanying PNG files are original required texture support. Keep PNGs beside the models. These are original visual props, not certified functional products.

## Limitations
Original visual prop, not an exact replica; 16 mm silent projector references and photo-inferred scale with roughly 15% uncertainty. 13808/10224/5236 triangles; nine BLEND/FBX/GLB exports reopened. Overlapping face-projection UVs and four solid-color PBR materials, not a baked atlas. Only lens moves +X by authored 2 mm over frames 1-31; reels and other parts static, no functional mechanism. Validation samples half frames, UV/material presence and zero-area faces. Spindle negative control renames rather than physically deletes geometry. Contact graph includes an unconditional sleeve-barrel edge and is not physical-retention proof; separate approximate sleeve checks do not establish engineering safety. Limited wheel checks, no blanket collision-free claim. GLB import has seam-split topology; not a watertight manufacturing mesh. Anonymous recognition was initial blockout only, parent-relayed, not independent final-model recognition. No raw-normal/full-material equivalence, optical, mechanical or Unity certification claimed.
