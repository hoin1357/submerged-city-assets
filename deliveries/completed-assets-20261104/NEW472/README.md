# NEW472: handheld holmes stereoscope

Three LODs, each in BLEND, FBX and GLB (nine model files). Any accompanying PNG files are original required texture support. Keep PNGs beside the models. These are original visual props, not certified functional products.

## Limitations
Original handheld Holmes-type stereoscope visual interpretation, not exact replica; inferred envelope 0.178 x 0.310 x 0.214 m, 10064/4260/1996 triangles and 28 mesh objects per LOD. Nine actual reopens pass exact per-component geometry and supplied UV/normal/raw GLB/PBR/embedded-image checks. Four-second clip: focus carrier 20 mm along +Y and grip fold 80 degrees; author checks 17 normalized samples, independent focused audit five checkpoints plus native rail/end-stop surface probes. Supplied comprehensive aggregates were reviewed independently, not all rerun independently. Three raw combined strict native diagnostics remain false due to separate-part attachment intersections; no union/global strict or complete static-contact pass. Tiled overlapping UVs, no unique lightmap atlas. Square alpha lens proxies and original card art, no optical prescription or validated stereo pair. R05 blind recognition about 90% transferred only to identical decoded R06 gray-view pixels; no fresh R06 blind test. No continuous collision, physical/load-bearing, fabrication, Unity or Unreal certification.
