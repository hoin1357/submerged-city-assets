# NEW473: tabletop hand operated platen letterpress

Three LODs, each in BLEND, FBX and GLB (nine model files). Any accompanying PNG files are original required texture support. Keep PNGs beside the models. These are original visual props, not certified functional products.

## Limitations
Original tabletop platen letterpress visual prop. Four-second impression stroke only, not a full printing cycle: rollers remain parked and there is no ink transfer, disc indexing, paper deformation or printing simulation. Native clamped stroke drivers and coordinated baked export clips are preserved. Intentional seating overlaps mean this is not a unioned or globally collision-free assembly. The original 0.2 micrometre neutral-position comparison FAILS on all three FBX routes, maximum 0.202677704524 micrometre. A reviewer-selected supplemental 1 micrometre comparison passes but is not user-approved and does not replace the original failure. Other component/attribute and sampled motion checks have bounded scope, not continuous collision, load-bearing, manufacturing or engineering certification. Tiled UVs are not a unique lightmap atlas. No live-engine test is claimed.
