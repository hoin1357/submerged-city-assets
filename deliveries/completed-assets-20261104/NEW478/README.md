# NEW478: original freestanding pedestal birdbath

Three LODs, each in BLEND, FBX and GLB (nine model files). Any accompanying PNG files are original required texture support. Keep PNGs beside the models. These are original visual props, not certified functional products.

## Limitations
Original unbranded pedestal birdbath, one static garden visual prop. Nine model files, LOD triangles 5184/2592/912, one closed positive-volume continuous mesh from foot through stem to open concave basin, one material and original embedded base-color texture. Estimated 0.562 x 0.562 x 0.685 m dimensions; exact dimensions, underside, monolithic join and material are modeling estimates. Independently measured floor-to-lip depth 56 to 62 mm; samples establish cavity/wall geometry, not exhaustive minimum thickness or fluid behavior. Author-selected 0.05 degree cross-format normal tolerance established before export, not user-approved criteria or bitwise equality. Strongest blind-review alternative is a decorative pedestal serving bowl without scale context. No water, fountain, plumbing, bird, rig, animation, physical stability, collision proxy, engine LOD group, manufacturing or live-engine certification. Diagnostic cutaway is preview-only. Manufacturer photos/HTML remain private.
