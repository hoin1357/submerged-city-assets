# NEW501: closed pressed-steel jerrycan

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Closed static exterior prop only: no rig, opening mechanism, liquid interior, leakproofness, fuel transport, load or safety certification.
- Manufacturer nominal envelope 345×165×468 mm is reference anchor; rib profiles, wall gauge, cap details and underside are artistic estimates. Underside not photographed; no functional internal reconstruction.
- LOD1 and especially LOD2 intentionally soften pressed ribs, simplify curved borders and omit tiny parts. Low tier is for distance, not close inspection. No engine runtime/LOD switch test.
- Measured Blender GLB reimport normal exception exceeds initial tolerance; raw exported normals remain within original bound. No bit-exact normals claim.
