# NEW508: wood-handled coping saw

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Static visual prop only. No cutting, blade-tension, rotation, strength, mechanical, ergonomic, manufacturing or tool-safety certification.
- Concealed overlaps remain among frame ends, eyes, axles, hub, grip and pin/blank engagements. No interference-free moving assembly or resolved hidden threading.
- Only overall 300 mm length,165 mm blade blank, 125 mm height and 14 TPI are manufacturer-dimensional grounding; detailed gauges and hardware are estimates.
- LOD0 and LOD1 use 84 connected simplified triangular tooth tips over a shortened toothed region. Exact manufacturer tooth count, alternating tooth set, grinding, rake and cutting profile are not validated.
- The 0.5 mm blade and fine teeth can disappear or alias at small screen size or near edge-on views; supplied views demonstrate visibility, not all-angle or runtime guarantees.
- LOD0 retains geometric facets in macro views. LOD1 has clearly coarser curved hardware and handle facets at close range. LOD2 has faceted frame/handle/hardware and omits serrations; use at distance.
- Scalar wood/steel PBR only; no photographic grain, wear maps, texture dependency or normal-map relief. UVs use separate triangle charts.
- Inspection datum is the lower carrier-seat plane, not a claim that the upright tool balances or rests naturally.
- Fresh Blender 4.3.2 import/render validation only; no universal importer, rasterizer, animation or target-engine performance claim.
