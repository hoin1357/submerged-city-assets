# NEW509: wooden through-handle rubber mallet

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Static visual game prop only. No physical impact, rebound, load, fit tolerance, self-locking performance, rubber hardness, material, tool-use, ergonomic, manufacturing or safety certification.
- The bore and wood have intentional buried radial interference. Hidden manufacturer attachment details are not reverse-engineered; no wedge, glue or friction mechanism is certified.
- Only approximate overall 340 mm, maximum diameter 64 mm and head axial length 113 mm are source-grounded nominal dimensions. Detailed dimensions and seam shape are estimates.
- LOD0 has visible geometric facets in macro views. LOD1 is more visibly faceted and LOD2 has strongly polygonal head/handle/end contours; use lower LODs at distance.
- Two scalar PBR surfaces only. Wood has no grain or end-grain texture; rubber has no photographed wear, microrelief or exact moulding irregularity. No external textures or normal-map relief.
- The handle-butt ground plane is an upright inspection datum, not proof the mallet will balance or rest naturally.
- Validation covers Blender 4.3.2 file import and the recorded camera views, not every renderer, angle, screen size, target engine, animation or runtime performance.
