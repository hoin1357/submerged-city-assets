# NEW510: fixed four-way wheel-nut wrench

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Static visual game prop only. No torque, load, lug-nut fitment, tool-use, material strength, ergonomic, manufacturing or safety certification.
- Four source-nominal across-flat widths and interpreted 380 mm full span are modeled. 23 mm cavity depth, wall profile, forged shoulders, flattening and hidden welded construction are estimates, not manufacturing tolerances.
- The locally flattened center is simplified and has no separately modeled irregular weld bead. Geometry is one fused component, but the blind visual alternative of two crossed double-ended spanners remains explicit.
- LOD0 shows fine facets and banded highlights at macro scale. LOD1 has coarser facets, and LOD2 has strongly polygonal shafts, shoulders and outer socket rims. Lower LODs are intended for small/distant use.
- One uniform blue scalar PBR surface only, without photographed enamel texture, stamped text, wear, irregular weld texture or normal-map relief. Rendered hue and gloss depend on lighting and are not colorimetric material certification.
- The min-Z ground plane is an inspection datum, not proof of a stable rest, balance or physical collision behavior.
- Validation covers Blender 4.3.2 imports and recorded views, not every renderer, angle, screen size, target engine or runtime performance. Geometric sampling and floating-point tolerances limit exhaustive guarantees.
