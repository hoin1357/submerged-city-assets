# NEW511: straight two-handle woodworking drawknife

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Static visual game prop only. No cutting, sharpness, safety, tool function, material hardness/strength, ergonomic, manufacturing or physical collision certification.
- Only central 200 mm cutting span is manufacturer verified. All remaining dimensions, exact bevel angle,0.28 mm finite edge land, hidden tang, grip bores and attachment method are estimates, not manufacturing tolerances.
- Tangs have sampled clearance in the wood bores and are joined to the end fittings by bounded visual overlaps. Fittings overlap the wood ends. These relationships are not a valid press-fit design, fastener specification or mechanical assembly proof.
- LOD0 has visible facets and highlight bands in macro views, especially grips and arm bends. LOD1 is coarser; LOD2 has strongly polygonal grips, fittings and bends and is intended for small/distant use.
- Five scalar PBR materials only. No photographed wood grain, varnish irregularity, forge scale, grind scratches, logos, wear or normal-map relief. Hue and gloss depend on lighting.
- The minimum-Z stage is an inspection datum, not proof of stable rest, balance or physical behavior. The supplied object is uncovered; no leather guard is modeled.
- Validation covers Blender 4.3.2 fresh imports and recorded views, not every renderer, angle, screen size, target engine or runtime performance. Sampled geometry and floating-point tolerances limit exhaustive guarantees.
