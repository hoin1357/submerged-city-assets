# NEW514: rattan carpet and rug beater

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- The 0.750 m length is grounded in the Redecker 349400 manufacturer listing; 0.250 m maximum width is an approximate same-SKU retailer dimension, not a manufacturer engineering tolerance. Actual modeled depth is approximately 21.66–21.88 mm and is artist-estimated.
- The full backside weave, reed diameter, twist pitch, transition bends, binding and blind ferrule details are original photographic estimates. The regularized open-loop construction is category-consistent but is not an exact manufacturer weave, reconstruction, mechanical specification or manufacturing CAD.
- The model has 44,296 / 17,594 / 8,048 triangles. It is a complex woven prop, not a lightweight asset or a guarantee of target-platform performance. Close-up faceting is visible, especially at the lower LODs. LOD2 uses four-sided reed sections and needs a distant/small-screen role.
- Only Blender 4.3.2 import and CPU rendering plus direct GLB payload decoding were validated. No Unity, Unreal, other target engine, target-device frame budget, runtime lighting, collision, rigging, animation or deformation validation was performed.
- Four scalar PBR materials are preserved without photo textures, grain, weathering, image dependencies or normal-map texture details. UV0 is globally nonoverlapping and finite but is not claimed as a hand-authored texture-painting or lightmap solution.
- The geometric tests are static numerical checks. Geometric vertex identity uses 1e-7 m rounding. Strict self-intersection tests exclude shared-vertex adjacency and exact point/edge tangencies. Component triangle clearance is double-precision and exceeds 1e-7 m in all tested pairs, but does not certify dynamic collision, material flexibility, strength, friction, fastening or safe physical use.
- The smallest actual component surface gap is about 0.068903 mm at the ferrule. These deliberately small clearances are visual estimates, not manufactured-fit tolerances. The head grid sampled real separated over/under layers; it is not an exhaustive crossing map. One LOD2 silhouette ray in each format skipped a sub-0.1-micrometer solid interval at its fixed marching offset, resolved by independent double-precision full triangle intersections.
- Prior 88% confidence at 320 px and 97% at full size belong to the original gray prototype only. The final repaired/lighter geometry was reviewed with knowledge of the category and compared visually; those scores were neither repeated nor transferred to final files.
