# NEW515: handheld serrated bulb planter

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Manufacturer-grounded total length is 290 mm, maximum cutter-head width 70 mm and nominal soil core 60 mm. The retailer 280 mm overall conflict is retained, with the manufacturer selected. The 70 mm is not grip span; the 60 mm is not an engineering bore tolerance.
- Tube height, grip span/diameter, taper, 1.5 mm radial wall, twelve tooth count/depth/lands, support shapes and fixing heads are photo-informed artist estimates. This is an original unbranded visual interpretation, not an exact manufacturer reconstruction.
- The four closed positive material shells include one through-open cutter/support shell, separate wood, and two heads. Exactly three object pairs have intentional planar seat contact. Concealed shanks/holes are omitted; visible seating does not certify fastening, joint strength, cutting function, fit, ergonomics or safety.
- Geometric vertex identity rounds at 1e-7 m. Self-intersection broad phase and strict tests exclude shared-vertex adjacency and point/edge tangencies. UV overlap uses a positive area threshold of 1e-12. Contact-plane membership uses 1e-7 m tolerance; actual separated-halfspace gaps are additionally tested at 1e-12 m and are exactly zero in these imported data.
- The smallest geometric through-cutter cylinder is 59.5654379 mm across the nine actual files; this includes polygon chord approximation. The 333 axial test rays traverse the cutter to Z=220 mm, below the wood grip, not through the entire tool height. The modeled lower-bore vertex diameter is 60 mm.
- Four scalar PBR materials have no image textures, grain, weathering, external dependencies or normal-map texture detail. UV0 is finite, bounded and globally nonoverlapping at the stated threshold, but is not claimed as a hand-painted atlas or lightmap solution.
- Only Blender 4.3.2 fresh imports, independent CPU renders and raw GLB binary decoding were validated. No target-engine, target-device performance, animation, deformation, runtime lighting or collision testing was performed. Faceting, particularly LOD2, is visible in close views; LOD2 needs a small-screen/distant role.
