# NEW521: broad flat decorating paintbrush

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Static unbranded visual prop only. No functional, manufacturing, physical safety, durability, motion, target-engine, or runtime integration certification.
- Manufacturer evidence covers 76.2 mm nominal head width, 22.225 mm head depth, and 87.3125 mm exposed bristles. 276.225 mm overall is secondary retailer-snippet corroboration, not a manufacturer specification. Other details and scalar material parameters are visual estimates.
- Nine separate closed bodies have finite-area tangent seats; this is not a fabricated hollow ferrule or modeled hidden fastener/joinery mechanism.
- LOD2 has angular handle/hole geometry and very subdued bristle relief; use as a distant-view LOD rather than a close-up hero. LOD0/1 also remain deliberately stylized, scalar-PBR, untextured assets.
- Vertex weld is analysis-only at 10 nm for contacts and 100 nm for metadata; raw source geometry is unchanged. Shared intentional contact boundaries are not counted as interpenetration.
- UV overlap criterion >1e-12 normalized area, geometric area threshold >1e-12 m 2 in metadata, position/UV export tolerance 1e-6, normal tolerance 0.01 degree and 1e-4 component.
- Visual files are low-sample controlled audit renders of reopened assets, not an independent photoreal material or real-world finish certification.
