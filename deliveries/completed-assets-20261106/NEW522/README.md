# NEW522: empty ribbed paint-roller tray

Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- Static original unbranded visual prop only. No real-world function, roller fit, capacity, strength, manufacturing, durability, safety, motion, target-engine or runtime integration certification.
- Hamilton 3762201-90 photographs establish broad form. Manufacturer 9-inch label means nominal roller compatibility 228.6 mm, not external tray width. The authored 375×285×60 mm envelope is secondary evidence: exact-SKU LiveMoor indexed result whose direct original returned 403, corroborated by a TradeKart set tray page without standalone SKU equivalence. No manufacturer-exact dimensional claim.
- Hidden underside, integral feet, wall thickness, chevron count/relief, recesses, notch and subordinate details are generic authored estimates, not reverse-engineered manufacture.
- Deliberately low-poly scalar-PBR asset: angular reservoir transition and rear supports, restrained thin relief, no texture maps. LOD2 is for distant game-scale use; no close-up hero or smoothly molded surface claim.
- Single UV0 has finite nonoverlapping charts in 0–1. LOD0 and repaired LOD2 use separated triangle charts suited to scalar materials, with high seam count; no optimized paintable/lightmap/texel-density guarantee.
- LOD reduction is geometric and visual only, not a promise of smaller export files, GPU memory, draw calls or engine performance. Repaired LOD2 GLB is 90,040 bytes versus LOD1 57,096 because separate UV charts split export vertices.
- All 1,735 initial near-parallel triangle flags were individually resolved with exact rational plane sections: 1,158 common topology, 485 absent exact sections, 92 disjoint intervals. Initial detector incorrectly treated near-plane vertices as exact endpoints. Corrected section test and original raw results retained; no unexplained flags waived.
- Intersection tolerance 20 nm coplanar classification, 100 nm crossing length, 1e-12 m² coplanar area; raw exact flag diagnosis uses no tolerance. Sub-tolerance geometry remains unresolved. Analysis-only weld 10 nm for contact topology, 100 nm for metadata; models unchanged.
- 77 rays per file sample open well, slope, chevrons, recesses, notch, thickness and ground support; they are not a continuous thickness or physical-performance certification.
- Export corner tolerances remain 1e-6 m position, 1e-6 UV, 0.01 degree and 1e-4 normal component, 1e-6 scalar material. Direct raw GLB accessor decoding verifies stored normals independently of importer.
- Prior blind gray prototype assessment is provenance only. Final 90-image review is informed, low-sample controlled rendering, not a new blind test or photoreal finish certification.
