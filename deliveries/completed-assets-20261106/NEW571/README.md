Original static visual 3 D prop. Nine model files: LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2, each supplied as BLEND, FBX and GLB. Only the accepted final static models are included.

## Materials and use

The models use embedded scalar PBR materials. No external textures, material companion files, manufacturer photographs or reference PDFs are required or included. Blender import checks do not certify every engine or renderer. No rig, animation, physical performance, safety, manufacturing or exact commercial-product replica is guaranteed. Lower LODs may show deliberate faceting.

UV layouts are provided. Many use separated triangle charts; continuous paint-ready unwrapping and universal lightmap/baking suitability are not guaranteed.

## Asset-specific scope and limitations

- One complete sealed static body with four thick wrapped straps. Source nominal open envelope 655 × 385 × 485 mm; hidden construction and parts are inferred. Maker/retailer folded-depth discrepancy remains unresolved. No folded model, articulation, cloth simulation or load certification.
- The accepted revision includes bounded internal retopology. Saved vertices, original polygon boundaries and corner UV/material/part assignments are preserved, but exact interior surface and interpolated-UV equality to the preceding revision is FALSE. Maximum measured surface changes are 12.3898 / 8.5854 / 8.8764 nanometres by LOD. It is not mathematically unchanged.
- GLB intentionally omits optional NORMAL/TANGENT attributes. Consumers must derive flat normals as specified by glTF 2.0 §3.7.2.1. Default Blender imports meet the 0.01-degree normal gate; universal importer behavior is not certified.
